TagAlong – Delete Your Account

Last updated: October 22, 2026

TagAlong, developed by Jason Smith, lets you delete your account and associated data at any time.

How to Delete Your Account

  • In the app (immediate): open TagAlong, go to Settings, and tap Delete Account. You will be asked to confirm your password or Google account, and your account is deleted straight away.
  • By email: if you can no longer use the app, send a request to jasonsmithdev.117@gmail.com from the email address registered to your account, with the subject "Account Deletion Request."

What Gets Deleted

  • Your sign-in account (Firebase Authentication)
  • Your profile: name, email address and profile photo
  • Every trip you host, and everything in it: photos, chat messages, expenses and repayments, calendar events, polls and packing lists
  • Your membership of trips hosted by other people

What May Be Retained

  • Content you added to trips hosted by other people (such as messages, photos, expenses, poll votes and packing items) stays visible to that trip's remaining members, under the name you used, because it is part of the group's shared record. It is deleted when the host deletes the trip. You can delete your own photos, expenses and events from a trip before deleting your account, or ask us by email to remove specific content.
  • Google may keep advertising and app-distribution data under its own policies.

Processing Time

In-app deletion happens immediately. We process email requests within 30 days of receipt and will reply to confirm once your account and data have been deleted.

Contact

Questions about this process can be sent to jasonsmithdev.117@gmail.com.
