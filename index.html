TagAlong – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

TagAlong ("we", "our", or "us") is a group trip planning app developed by Jason Smith. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, who can see it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, a unique user ID and, if you add one, a profile photo. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile photo from your Google account. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us.
  • Trip content you and your group add: trip details (name, destination, dates, cover photo and optional budget), calendar events, expenses and recorded repayments between members (amounts, who paid and how they are split), photos and captions, polls and votes, and packing lists.
  • WhatsApp group link: if a trip's host links a WhatsApp group, we store that group's invite link so the trip's members can open it. To link it, the host copies the invite link in WhatsApp, and TagAlong reads the clipboard to pick it up, only after the host has started linking a group. Nothing else from the clipboard is kept. Group conversations happen in WhatsApp: TagAlong never sees your WhatsApp messages, contacts or phone number.
  • Photos: only the photos you choose with the photo picker or take with the camera. They are resized and compressed on your device before upload. The app does not access the rest of your photo library.
  • Advertising and device data: TagAlong shows banner ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads, in order to show and measure ads, which may be personalised. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), Google's consent form asks for your choice before ads use your data, and you can change it at any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Crash reports: if the app crashes or hits an error, Firebase Crashlytics collects technical details such as your device model, Android version, app version, the error and the steps leading up to it, tagged with your TagAlong user ID (not your name or email). We use this only to find and fix bugs.
  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, we store a push token for each phone you sign in on (a random ID from Firebase Cloud Messaging that lets us reach that device) with your account. We use it only to tell you about your trips: new expenses you share in, payments to or from you, new polls, people joining, the trip starting or ending, and a reminder the day before it starts. The token is removed when you sign out, and you can turn trip notifications off in Settings → Notifications or in Android's settings.
  • Fingerprint unlock: if you turn on fingerprint sign-in, the check is done by Android on your device. The app only learns whether it succeeded; your fingerprint data never leaves your device and is never sent to us.
  • Preferences: settings such as your theme and fingerprint choice are stored only on your device.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or payment card details. TagAlong does not process payments: repayments recorded in the Costs tab are notes only, and money moves outside the app.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in
  • To provide the app's features: trips, invite codes, shared calendars, costs, photos, polls, packing lists and the link to your trip's WhatsApp group
  • To show your name and profile photo to the members of trips you join
  • To show ads, which keep TagAlong free
  • To prevent abuse and keep the service working

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Who Can See Your Information

  • Trip members: everything added to a trip, together with your name and profile photo, is visible to all members of that trip. Anyone with a trip's invite code can join it while it is open, so only share codes with people you trust. The host can remove members and reset the invite code at any time.
  • Your email address is not shown to other users.
  • Service providers listed below process data on our behalf to run the app.
  • We may disclose information if required by law.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Google): sign-in, the cloud database, photo storage, push notifications (Cloud Messaging) and crash reporting (Crashlytics) — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google AdMob: advertising — policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Google Sign-In and Google Play: sign-in and app distribution — policies.google.com/privacy

Trip group chats take place in WhatsApp, a separate service operated by Meta — whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy. Members of a WhatsApp group can see each other's phone numbers there. The Accommodation tab opens searches on Booking.com, Airbnb and Google Hotels in your browser. These services are not part of TagAlong and their own privacy policies apply.

5. Where Your Data Is Processed

  • South Africa: your profile and trip data are stored in Google Cloud's Johannesburg region.
  • Google's global infrastructure: sign-in (Firebase Authentication), photo storage, crash reporting (Crashlytics) and advertising (AdMob) are operated by Google, which may process this information in other countries, including the United States.

The data protection laws of these countries may differ from those where you live. All data is encrypted in transit, and access rules ensure trip content can only be read by members of that trip. By using TagAlong, you consent to your information being processed in these countries.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

  • We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. Crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
  • When a trip's host deletes the trip, everything in it (photos, expenses, events, polls, packing lists and the WhatsApp group link) is permanently deleted. The WhatsApp group itself is managed in WhatsApp.
  • If you leave a trip, what you added to it stays visible to its remaining members, because it is part of the group's shared record.
  • You can delete your account at any time in the app under Settings → Delete Account, or by request. See Delete Account for exactly what is deleted.

7. Children's Privacy

TagAlong is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, including under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and, where it applies, the EU/UK GDPR, you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information and to object to how it is processed. You can edit your name and photo in the app under Settings, delete your account in the app, or contact us at the email below for anything else. You may also lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa or your local data protection authority.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continuing to use TagAlong after an update means you accept the updated policy.
